About

Hi! I'm Andreea.

I'm a Copywriter & Social Media Content Creator. I help businesses communicate their value through engaging promotional copy and social media content that captures attention, connects with their audience, and encourages action.

What I can help you with

  • Ads. A ready-to-publish ad: headline, text and a clear next step for the reader. Each ad starts from a problem your customer feels and leads to your offer.
  • Social media posts. Instagram and Facebook posts, carousels (posts with several images), captions and Reels scripts (scripts for short videos).
  • Product, service & event texts. Descriptions of your products and services, and announcements for your events.

My approach

I believe effective communication starts with understanding the audience. Good content doesn't just fill a social media feed. It gives people a reason to stop, pay attention, and connect with a business.

I combine creative thinking, copywriting techniques, audience research, and modern AI tools to develop content that is clear, relevant, and aligned with each business's message.

Every project starts with understanding your business, your audience, and what you want to communicate. I value clear communication, attention to detail, and thoughtful collaboration.
