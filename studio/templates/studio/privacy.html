Privacy

What I do with your details

What I collect

Only what you type in the free ad form: your name, email, business name, what you sell, a link to your social media or website (if you add one), your goal (if you add one) and your message.

Why

Only to reply to your request and to write your free ad or your package. I do not use your details for anything else, and I do not sell or share them.

Where they are stored

On the server that hosts this website. Only I can see them, from a private admin area.

How long I keep them

Up to 12 months after your request, then I delete them. You can ask me to delete them earlier.

Cookies and fonts

  • This website uses only the technical cookie needed for the form to work. There are no tracking or advertising cookies.
  • The fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google when a page loads.
